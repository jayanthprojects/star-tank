Star Tanks

Pick your ship, then choose a mode.

Arrows or WASD to fly, Space to fire. Asteroids break in two hits, planets are solid. Every ship has 20 health, so 20 hits eliminates it. Up to 10 pilots share a room code: Individual is free-for-all, Team splits 5v5 with no friendly fire.
